2. WHAT ARE COOKIES?
Cookies are small text files that are placed and stored on a user's device (including computers, tablets, smartphones, and other internet-enabled devices) when visiting a website or using a digital service.

Cookies enable websites and systems to:

Recognize returning users;
Maintain session information;
Authenticate users;
Remember preferences and settings;
Analyze website performance;
Improve security;
Personalize content and services;
Measure marketing effectiveness;
Detect fraud and abuse.
Cookies do not typically contain information that directly identifies an individual. However, where cookies are linked to an account, IP address, device identifier, or other personal information, they may constitute personal information or personal data under applicable laws.
